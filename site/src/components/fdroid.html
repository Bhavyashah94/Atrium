Atrium was built to fill the niche left by LunaSea. It ships with reproducible builds, no proprietary blobs and no telemetry. Licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later, it’s a modern Flutter codebase that fully respects your privacy.

Because that build is reproducible, F-Droid rebuilds Atrium from source, checks the result matches the published APK byte for byte, and ships it under this project’s own signature. The two sources are interchangeable, so you can move between F-Droid and the releases page without uninstalling.
