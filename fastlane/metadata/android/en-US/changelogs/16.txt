Two new services this time. Beszel shows your systems with live metrics and a
detailed screen for each. Dashdot shows live CPU, memory, disk, and GPU usage
with a system information tab.

qBittorrent gained tag management: filter your torrents by tag and copy a
tracker URL from the trackers list. The Files tab now shows the full file name.

Fixed: testing a connection now checks your credentials for Beszel and for
qBittorrent API keys, the Deluge detail screen no longer crashes on some
torrents, and scrolling is smoother in Emby, Jellyfin, and Beszel.
