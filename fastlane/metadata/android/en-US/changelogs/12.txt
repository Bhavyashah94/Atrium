Deluge, Transmission and rTorrent are now services you can add, so every common
torrent client is covered alongside qBittorrent. Each gives you a live torrent
list with start, stop, remove, recheck and reannounce, filter chips and sorting,
global speed limits, and a detail screen with files, peers and trackers. Add a
torrent by magnet link, .torrent URL or file, and pick where it lands.

Tracearr is here too: who is streaming right now, watch and library statistics,
history, and a map of where accounts are being used from.

The torrent screens lead with the two live speeds, and a transfer that is
actually moving gets a wavy progress bar so you can tell at a glance which ones
are running. A finished torrent shows its share ratio rather than sitting at
100 percent saying nothing. Detail screens now open over the whole screen
instead of inside the tab.

Fixed a refresh loop where a screen slower to load than its own refresh interval
would restart forever and never finish loading, which mostly hit people on slow
or remote connections. A server that stays unreachable no longer flashes a
spinner over the error every few seconds.

Atrium also builds for iOS now. There is no download, since the licence rules
out the App Store, so you build and sideload it yourself.
