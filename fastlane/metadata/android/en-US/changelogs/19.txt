Lidarr is now a supported service. Artists and discography, album and track
detail, wanted, queue and history, the full settings tree, an in-app log reader,
and album releases in the calendar. It is marked beta while it settles in.

qBittorrent gained a settings page covering what its own web interface offers,
across system, behaviour, downloads, connection, speed, BitTorrent, RSS, web UI
and advanced. Torrents can also be moved around the queue and sorted by queue
position, and the screen now leads with your totals and what is seeding.

The app icon follows your system palette when Android themed icons are on.

Changing a qBittorrent setting that would cut Atrium off from the server now
asks first, and says what it will do.
